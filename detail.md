# Initial Taught of the app

I want to create an online wardrobe and outfit selection application under the concept "easy to use." The application will feature an online wardrobe where users can upload photos of their clothes to be automatically categorized into groups like hats, tops, pants, shoes, and accessories, with options to name the items or add brand tags. It will include an automated outfit generator that can also suggest outfits based on specific user preferences, such as personal color, daily lucky colors, current weather conditions, and desired styles. There will be an "in a rush" mode that instantly picks out a single outfit for busy days, as well as a repetition tracker that alerts users if they have been wearing the same style or color tone too frequently. Additionally, the app will recommend missing pieces that users should have or accessories to complement their wardrobe, and it will feature an AI chatbot to converse with users about fashion, such as identifying the name of a specific style. To ensure personalized recommendations, the app will learn individual user preferences, starting with an onboarding style quiz before entering the main interface.

For the UX/UI direction, the app will support both light mode and dark mode, featuring a design blend of liquid glass and Material UI, along with a floating bottom navigation bar containing four main menus. The first is the Home Screen, which centers around a customizable 3D avatar where users can choose whether it is a human or a standing pet like a dog or cat; this screen will also have a profile icon in the top left that navigates to the profile page and a settings button in the top right. The second is the Wardrobe Screen, which displays all uploaded clothing items organized by categories like hats, tops, pants, shoes, and accessories, featuring an upload button in the bottom left and a search bar to find clothes by their custom names. The third is the Missing Piece Screen, which provides recommendations on what items users should add to their collection, and the fourth is the AI Chatbot Screen. For the backend direction, the app will include a login system that syncs across multiple devices, supporting Google and Facebook authentication, with Supabase used as the database. The entire app will be developed using Flutter to ensure easy multi-platform deployment.
