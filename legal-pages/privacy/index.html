Information you provide

MMM can store your profile and wardrobe information, including clothing photos, item details, outfits, wear history, preferences, and messages you send to Fashion Chat. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Google, or enabled Facebook sign-in, MMM receives the account information supplied by that provider to create and maintain your account.

How information is stored and used

Guest profile and wardrobe data stay on your device unless you sign in. For signed-in accounts, MMM stores account, wardrobe, outfit, preference, activity, consent, and chat data in Supabase. Wardrobe images are uploaded to the private MMM Supabase storage bucket for signed-in synchronization. MMM uses this information to provide wardrobe organization, outfit recommendations, history, and account features.

Optional AI features

Third-party AI features require your explicit, versioned consent and can be revoked in Settings. With consent, MMM may send the information needed for the requested feature to its configured AI provider: wardrobe images for cloud image analysis; wardrobe, profile, weather, and wear-history information for AI outfit and missing-piece suggestions; and the message plus the documented color-season style field for Fashion Chat. Without consent, cloud AI features are unavailable or MMM uses its documented local or deterministic alternatives.

Device permissions and on-device processing

MMM requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to add an image, and location access only when you choose weather matching. On-device image analysis may use Apple Vision on iOS or Google ML Kit on Android; MMM does not send images to a classification service for that local processing.

Service providers

MMM uses Supabase for authenticated account data, database, and storage services. Optional cloud AI requests use the configured AI provider. Weather matching uses the configured weather service. These providers process information only to provide their services to MMM.

Retention and deletion

Guest data remains on the device until you remove it or delete the app, subject to your device backup settings. You can delete a signed-in account in Settings. MMM then removes wardrobe storage objects and deletes the account; related database records are deleted through the account relationships. Apple authorization is revoked when applicable. Temporary Apple deletion recovery data is designed to expire within ten minutes. Legal, security, and backup retention requirements may require limited retention where applicable.

Your choices

You can use MMM as a guest, decline or revoke AI consent, control device permissions through your device settings, sign out, and delete your signed-in account. Contact the operator at the address above for privacy requests once the operator contact is completed.

Changes

MMM may update this policy when its practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
